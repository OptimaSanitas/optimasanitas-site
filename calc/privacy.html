Seeker Mobile Calc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 03, 2026 · Operator: Optima Sanitas

Seeker Mobile Calc is a scientific calculator for Solana Mobile Seeker with optional Solana read-only tools.

What we do not collect

No accounts, no login, no analytics SDK. All expressions and history stay on your device unless you manually copy them.

What the App may use or display

1. Public Solana RPC calls (slot, balance, transactions).
2. Optional wallet connection via Mobile Wallet Adapter (you sign; keys stay in Seed Vault).
3. Optional public quote API (no wallet data sent).

On-chain data

Blockchain data is public by design. We do not delete on-chain records.
